Common Questions

What counts as one correction? ▼

Each essay submission that receives AI feedback counts as one correction, regardless of length or task type.

Can I pay in my local currency? ▼

Yes. Stripe automatically converts to your local currency at checkout — you'll see the exact amount before paying.

How do credits work? ▼

Credits are deducted one per essay correction. They never expire, so you can use them at any pace.

Can I cancel my subscription? ▼

Yes, any time. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
