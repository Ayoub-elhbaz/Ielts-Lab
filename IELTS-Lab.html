This is a real student essay, anonymised. The AI identified three issues across Lexical Resource, Task Achievement, and Grammatical Range. These are the before and after — after two AI sessions.

Task 2 — Opinion Essay
Some people believe children should spend more time outdoors. Do you agree or disagree?
5.5
Original — Band 5.5
Before IELTS Lab

I think that children should spend more time outside because it is very good for their health. Nowadays, many children stay at home and play video games all the time. This is a big problem because they do not get enough exercise.

In my opinion, parents should make their children go outside every day. If children play outside, they will be happy and healthy. In conclusion, I think outdoor activities are very important for children.

8 phrases flagged by AI examiner
7.5
Revised — Band 7.5
After 2 AI sessions

It is widely argued that children should dedicate a greater portion of their leisure time to outdoor pursuits, and I strongly concur with this view. In an era of pervasive digital entertainment, sedentary lifestyles among young people have become an increasingly pressing public health concern.

Encouraging children to engage in outdoor physical activity not only promotes cardiovascular health and fosters psychological wellbeing, but also develops crucial interpersonal skills through cooperative play. In light of these benefits, it is imperative that parents and educators prioritise structured outdoor experiences as an integral component of childhood development.

8 upgrades applied — every one is learnable
+1.5
Lexical Resource
"Very good" became "increasingly pressing." "Make children go" became "encourage children to engage." Every swap raises the band ceiling — and each one is a learnable pattern.
+1.0
Task Achievement
The original stated a position but gave no supporting logic. The revision builds a layered argument — health, psychology, social development — which is what examiners look for at Band 7+.
+1.0
Grammatical Range
Simple "if-then" sentences replaced with complex noun phrases, non-finite clauses ("to engage in"), and hedged academic structures ("it is imperative that") — all markers of Band 7+ grammar.
