The Recycled Paper Production Process (9 Stages)
Type: Linear cyclical process diagram Flow: Left-to-right, two rows, connected by arrows Stages: 9 total
1
Collection

Waste paper (newspapers, cardboard, office paper) gathered from homes, offices & recycling points into collection lorries.

↓
2
Sorting

Paper sorted by grade and type on a conveyor belt. Non-paper items (plastic, food waste) are removed.

↓
3
PulpingInput: water + chemical solvents

Paper enters a large vat. Agitation breaks it into a wet fibre slurry.

↓
4
ScreeningOutput: staples, clips, glue removed

Pulp passes through fine mesh screens to remove remaining contaminants.

↓
5
De-InkingOutput: ink foam skimmed off

Air bubbles + flotation chemicals introduced. Ink particles attach to bubbles and are removed as foam.

↓
6
BleachingInput: hydrogen peroxide / oxygen bleach

De-inked pulp is brightened to the desired whiteness level.

↓
7
Sheet Formation

Clean pulp diluted with water, spread in a thin layer across a moving wire mesh belt. Water drains, leaving a wet fibre mat.

↓
8
Pressing & Drying

Wet mat passes through heavy rollers to squeeze out water, then through heated cylindrical dryers.

↓
9
Finished Product ♻

Dried paper wound into reels or cut into sheets, labelled and packaged. A dotted return arrow leads back to Stage 1 (cyclical process).
