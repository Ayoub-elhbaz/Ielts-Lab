💡 Guide

How to Get the Most from Practice Tests

Use these tests under real exam conditions for the highest score gains.

01
Set a timer
Writing: 60 minutes (20 min Task 1, 40 min Task 2). Reading: 60 minutes for all 40 questions. The built-in timer starts automatically.
02
No looking up answers
Treat it like the real exam. No dictionary, no notes. You can review everything after — but the score only counts if you earned it.
03
Study the feedback
Don't just check your score. For writing, read every criterion comment. For reading, review every wrong answer with the explanation panel open.
04
Track your progress
Every test saves your score automatically. Visit the Mock Tests dashboard to see your history and band score trend over time.
